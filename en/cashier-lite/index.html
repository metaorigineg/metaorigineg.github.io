Installation steps

  1. 1Download the program from «Direct download» or any server above and extract it (it is a RAR file — open it with WinRAR or 7-Zip).
  2. 2If Windows shows «Windows protected your PC», click More info → Run anyway (this is normal for any new program not from the Microsoft Store).
  3. 3Run «Meta Origin - Cashier Lite Setup.exe» and choose: the main PC (the one that holds the data) or another device on the network.
  4. 4Choose «14-day trial» or enter your license — the machine code appears in the setup wizard if you want to activate.
  5. 5Open the program, sign in, and start from the detailed guide inside it — or contact us and we will help.

System requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
  • 4 GB RAM or more (8 GB for the main PC on a large network)
  • At least 1 GB of free disk space
  • Network: devices on the same router/network as the main PC
  • Works with thermal and A4 invoice printers and barcode readers

FAQ

Is it for one PC?

Yes, a simple and fast cashier for one PC — suitable for small shops.

Are there offers and delivery?

Yes: delivery, offers and loyalty points.

Does it show how much I made today?

Yes, shift closing, daily reports and profits.

Is the program free?

You get a full 14-day trial with every screen; after that you need a license — message us on WhatsApp for prices that fit your business.

Does it work on more than one PC?

Yes: a main PC holds the data and other devices connect on the same network, and many programs also open from the browser and the phone.

Is my data safe?

Your data stays on your own computer, with automatic backups in two places (and encrypted backups to Google Drive or OneDrive), permissions for every employee and a log of every action.

Is the program in English too?

Yes, the programs work in Arabic and English and you can switch between them.
