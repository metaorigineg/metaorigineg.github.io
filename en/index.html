Why Meta Origin?

Included in all our programs

Arabic & English

Every program works in Arabic and English and you can switch at any time — invoices and reports follow the same language.

One PC, network or online

A single PC, a main PC with devices on the network, or from the browser and phone from anywhere.

Security & permissions

Permissions for every employee and a log of every action, with protection against money tampering (discounts, voids and credit need the manager).

Automatic backups

Automatic backups in two places plus encrypted backups to Google Drive or OneDrive, backup testing and an alert if a backup is late.

Your look, your brand

More than 45 themes in light and dark mode, different styles and menu layouts, and your shop's name and logo inside the program.

Reports & export

Detailed reports and dashboards, Excel and PDF export, invoices and statements on WhatsApp, and importing items from Excel.

Your phone in your hand

A manager page on the phone, a barcode reader using the phone camera, and a daily report by email.

Guides & videos

Step-by-step help for every screen inside the program, a PDF user guide, and explainer videos on YouTube.

Try before you buy

A full 14-day trial, plus a demo copy full of sample data to try every screen without touching your own data.

Support & updates

Installation, training and support on WhatsApp, and updates installed from inside the program without affecting your data.
